# Welcome Day Attraction Deck

The student home page now begins with a clickable **Explore the ICT Welcome Day** deck. Each shortcut uses smooth scrolling to move students to the corresponding activity: Spin the Tech Wheel, Human or AI?, Future 2040, the photo corner, ICT Display Quest, or the five subject studios.

| Activity | Student interaction | Student-facing outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Spin the Tech Wheel | Students spin a visual seven-segment wheel or choose a segment directly. | An age-appropriate challenge in AI, Cyber, Gaming, Coding, Robotics, Tech Trivia, or Lucky Spin, with a suggested reward label. |
| Human or AI? | Students choose between two visual scene cards. | Immediate feedback identifies the computer-generated future-city concept and reinforces thoughtful observation. |
| Future 2040 | Students select a technology area. | A personalized Class of 2040 future-career card using their stored TFA display name when available. |
| Boys ICT Photo Corner | Students select a future-tech sign. | A UAE/TFA Welcome Back photo backdrop updates to show the selected handheld-sign idea. |

The attraction deck was reviewed in the student page at desktop and **390 × 844** mobile viewports. It preserves the official bilingual school header, the existing ICT Display Quest, the supplied-resource question activities, and the five core subject studios. The automated suite validates the seven wheel categories, Human-or-AI answer configuration, future-career options, photo-corner signs, unique page-shortcut targets, and all wheel random positions; **25 tests** pass.
